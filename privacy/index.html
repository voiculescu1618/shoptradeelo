Privacy Policy

Tradeelo POD Publisher — Last updated: 2026-05-13

Overview

Tradeelo POD Publisher is an internal application used to authenticate authorized Tradeelo social media accounts and publish Tradeelo-owned product marketing content.

Data We Access

When an authorized operator connects TikTok, the app may receive OAuth authorization data and access only the TikTok permissions approved by that operator and TikTok. Requested access is limited to what is needed to publish content and verify the authorized account.

How We Use Data

  • To complete OAuth login and connect the authorized Tradeelo TikTok account.
  • To submit Tradeelo-owned videos, captions, and related post metadata through TikTok Content Posting API.
  • To troubleshoot publishing status and maintain an audit trail for internal operations.

Data Storage and Sharing

We do not sell personal data. We do not share TikTok user data with advertisers or unrelated third parties. Access tokens, if stored, are kept only for authorized Tradeelo operations and are protected from public access.

Retention and Deletion

OAuth tokens and operational records are kept only as long as required for the publishing workflow, security, and compliance. To request deletion or revocation, contact Tradeelo through our Etsy shop.

Third-Party Services

The app may interact with TikTok APIs, GitHub Pages for website hosting, and Etsy for shop contact. Use of TikTok is also governed by TikTok's own terms and policies.

Contact

Contact Tradeelo via our Etsy shop.
