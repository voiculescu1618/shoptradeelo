TikTok OAuth Login

Tradeelo POD Publisher

This page is the login entry point for authorized Tradeelo operators connecting TikTok through OAuth.

In production, the operator clicks the TikTok authorization button in the internal publishing tool, approves the requested scopes in TikTok, and returns to the callback URL to complete setup.

Public self-service login is not available because this is an internal Tradeelo application.
